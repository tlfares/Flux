# Changelog

## 1.1

### All platforms
- **In English too:** the app now speaks French and English, following the device's language.
- **Free channels from the US and the UK:** news, local news, weather, sports, films and series, entertainment, documentaries, lifestyle, kids and music, including BBC News and Al Jazeera English. A new setting shows only France, US or UK channels, or all of them with your country first. The default follows your region.
- **More free French channels:** LCP – Public Sénat, France TV Sport, Docs and Séries, INA, more France 3 regions (Corse ViaStella, NoA), new Outre-mer and Jeunesse categories, more news, sport, music and local channels, and more backup streams.
- **Pluto TV** shows the line-up for your country and uses Pluto TV's newer streaming address.
- **Arrange your categories:** reorder or hide live TV, films and series categories, per account and section. Hidden ones still appear under "All", and you can reset to the provider's order.
- **Subtitles:** pick or turn off subtitles on streams that have them. Off by default, and your choice is remembered.
- **Choose your tabs:** turn the Guide, Films and Series tabs on or off. The Guide tab only shows when there is guide data.
- **Clear history:** removes recent channels and resume points in one step.
- **Free channels settings:** with the free channels account, settings show the country picker and hide options that don't apply.

### iPhone, iPad & Mac
- **New Guide tab:** a scrolling grid of the next hours for every channel in a category, with a line for the current time. Tap a programme to watch the channel.
- **New category panel:** Favorites, Recent and All shortcuts, search, item counts and country flags. Tap Edit to reorder or hide categories.
- Subtitles button in the live player, in both normal and full screen.

### Apple TV
- **Long-press during live TV** to switch to another stream of the channel (remembered per channel) or choose subtitles.
- **Organize categories:** hold a category to move or hide it, or open "Organize" to rearrange them all.

### Android & Android TV
- **Redesigned phone and tablet app**, matching the iPhone app: upright live screen with the picture on top, glass buttons over the video, new category panel, Guide tab and the Inter font.
- **Custom DNS:** route lookups through Cloudflare, Google or Quad9 (DNS over HTTPS) when your router blocks your provider, with fallback to the network's DNS.
- **Android TV player:** frosted glass over the live picture behind the channel banner, press left to switch streams or choose subtitles, and a black screen while zapping instead of a frozen frame.
- **Android TV categories:** a category column you can reorder and hide entries in.
- **Android TV text fields** are easier with the remote: left and right move the cursor, "Next" goes to the following field.
- The language can also be set per app in Android 13+ settings.
